# Reliability-Aware-Heterogeneous-Photonic-Digital-Twin
We propose a reliability-aware photonic digital twin coupling thermal and coherent circuit models with conformal prediction for safe control. In synthetic III-V/silicon tests, it achieved 96% simultaneous margin coverage, 56.2% availability, and zero unsafe authorizations, while reducing qualification responses by over 100× versus a dense grid.
# Main Finding
At a target marginal unsafe-authorization probability of 5%, the proposed controller authorizes 562 of 1,000 independent synthetic devices and has no observed unsafe authorization. Its whole-candidate-set margin coverage is 96.00%. The Gaussian comparator also has no observed unsafe authorization and authorizes 580 devices; empirical dominance over that baseline is not claimed. A changed interface population invalidates stale decisions. Recommissioning and fresh population qualification recover only a smaller feasible operating region.
